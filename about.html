About NMC

Who We Are

NMC is a Category A public enterprise - commercial in operation, developmental in purpose.

National Maize Corporation

NMC is a Category A public enterprise under the Ministry of Agriculture, established under the companies law of Eswatini. It operates on commercial lines - viable and self-financing - while carrying a social and developmental responsibility: protecting the local grain industry from cheap imports and promoting commercial grain production towards national self-sufficiency in staple grains.

Vision

To be a catalyst of grains sovereignty in the Kingdom of Eswatini.

Mission

To foster a vibrant grains industry, producing for national self-sufficiency, reserves and export markets.
