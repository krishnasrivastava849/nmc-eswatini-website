Quality & compliance

NMC has held FSSC 22000 (Version 6) food safety certification continuously since 2019, and has competed in Eswatini's National Quality Awards since 2017.

  • 1st Runner-Up, Product of the Year: 2017, 2018, 2019, 2021
  • 2024: 2nd Runner-Up, Service of the Year
  • 2024: Most Improved Organisation

Depots & distribution centres

Four depots serve all four regions, bringing the market closer to farmers at harvest. Distribution centres in non-producing areas make staple grains accessible to the public. Contact us for the nearest location.
