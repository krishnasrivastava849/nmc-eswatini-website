Subsidy Programmes

Tractor Hire Services

Government subsidises 30% of every tractor hour - farmers pay 70% at a flat rate.

Alongside inputs, the subsidy programme offers subsidised tractor hire so local farmers get ploughing done timely, effectively and efficiently. Because NMC has a small tractor fleet, private tractors are also engaged on contract.

30%Government share
70%Farmer share

How to book

  1. Pay the flat rate at any Eswatini Bank branch.
  2. Take your deposit slip to the nearest Rural Development Area (RDA).
  3. NMC's Tractor Pool Coordinator registers you and completes your job card.
  4. The tractor driver takes the job card and ploughs your field.

Flat-rate prices are announced yearly by the Minister of Agriculture (usually August). Call (+268) 2518 7432 for the current rate.
