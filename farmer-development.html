Farmer Development

Contract farming, youth incubation, financing partners and field officers for maize and bean producers.

Maize & bean production

Eswatini needs about 140,000 t of maize a year but grows around 75,000 t; the 65,000 t gap costs roughly E250 million in imports. Dry beans are worse - imports make up over 85% of the ~7,000 t consumed (March 2025).

NMC supports producers with production planning, farmer profiling, training and technical guidance to improve yield, quality and consistency.

Contract Farming

Structured, reliable market access for maize and beans.

  • Maize: minimum 3 ha arable land
  • Beans: minimum 2 ha arable land
  • Guaranteed market, lower risk, technical support, finance access, transparent grading

Apply through a Farmer Development Officer or your nearest RDA.

Youth Incubation Programme Since 2024

Skills, resources and mentorship for youth agripreneurs.

  • Liswati aged 18-35
  • Contracted with NMC
  • 2-5 ha per individual, or up to 10 ha per group
  • Able to contribute 10-20% of funding
  • Willing to register for insurance (repayment includes an insurance fee)
