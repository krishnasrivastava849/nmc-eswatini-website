How the subsidy works

Six steps from application to harvest

  1. Apply - collect a form from your RDA, Tinkhundla centre, NMC depot or extension officer.
  2. Assessment - the Vetting Committee or extension officer reviews it.
  3. Verification - NMC verifies the assessed form.
  4. SMS result - every applicant is told if they were successful.
  5. Pay your contribution - bank, MTN MoMo or EFT.
  6. Collect inputs from an approved agro-dealer.
