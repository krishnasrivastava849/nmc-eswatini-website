Subsidy Programmes

Input Subsidy Programme

Government covers 50% of input costs for small-scale farmers on up to 1 hectare of white maize, sugar beans or sorghum.

The Input Subsidy Programme is a key national intervention to empower small-scale farmers and safeguard food security. NMC has managed it for the Ministry of Agriculture since 2018. Each year, usually in August, the Minister of Agriculture announces the farmer contribution prices for inputs and tractor hire.

Supported commodities: white maize, sugar beans and sorghum only.

Eligibility criteria

  • Submit an application to be part of the programme
  • Access to land of up to 1 hectare
  • Soil test results form part of the application
  • Stable income above E12,000 per month is not eligible
  • Input packages allocated by suitability of agro-ecological zone
  • Beneficiaries of other government farming programmes are not eligible
  • One beneficiary per nuclear family
  • Maximum 3 years, then graduate to other developmental funds

Registration process

  1. Get an application form from an RDA, Tinkhundla centre, NMC depot, this website or an extension officer.
  2. Submit it to the Vetting Committee or Extension Officer for initial assessment.
  3. The Extension Officer sends assessed forms to the NMC office for verification.
  4. All applicants receive an SMS saying whether they were successful.
  5. Successful applicants pay their required contribution.
  6. Collect inputs from approved agro-dealers.

Payment options

  • Eswatini Bank - all branches
  • MTN MoMo Pay using retail codes
  • Electronic Funds Transfer (banking details from NMC)
